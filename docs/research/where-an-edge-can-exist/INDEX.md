# R011 · Where can an edge still exist for this project?

**Status:** 📚 Studied · **Date:** 2026-10-24

What the field knows that this project had not acted on, Microsoft's RD-Agent(Q) read beside this
project, and the paths ranked by promise, with a test design and a kill rule.

- **[where-an-edge-can-exist.md](where-an-edge-can-exist.md)** — the study and its sources.

**Answers:** Is there a promising path to a trading edge? What do people know that we do not? How does
Microsoft's RD-Agent differ from this project, and do its results change anything?

**Outcome:** US large caps on public prices and news tone hold no edge that survives costs (our
experiments, Chen and Welch 2026, RD-Agent(Q)'s IC 0.016 on the NASDAQ 100). The one promising,
untested path is information that is slow to be priced (insider purchases first, then filing changes),
possibly in mid and small caps. Feeds [DL-317](../../design-log.md); the operator decides.
